SaaS cohort retention

A walkthrough of the monthly cohort pipeline on synthetic billing data: how accounts are grouped by start month, how retention and net revenue retention move, and the small automation that posts the result to Slack once the numbers reconcile.

⚠️
Dummy data only. Every account (company_001, …), segment and amount on this page is synthetic, generated by data/generate_dummy_data.py with a fixed seed. It does not describe any real company or customer. See the data profile below.

Net revenue retention per month

Revenue this month from accounts that already paid last month, divided by their revenue last month. Above 100% = expansion beats churn.

Retention by cohort

Share of each start-month cohort still paying after N months. Hover a cell for the value.

Refresh validation: before vs after

A month later the same report is rebuilt from a fresh extract. Old cohort numbers move. Every changed cell (outlined) is traced to the invoices that caused it, and the run only counts as valid when the two totals reconcile. Click an outlined cell or a row.
